Lifting Logger Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 30, 2026

Overview

Lifting Logger is designed to help you track workouts and exercise progress. This privacy policy explains how information is handled when you use the app.

Information You Provide

The app may store workout information you choose to enter, such as exercises, sets, repetitions, weights, rest-timer settings, and training history. This information is used to provide workout tracking, volume calculations, progress history, and progressive-overload reminders.

How Information Is Used

Your workout information is used only to operate and improve Lifting Logger's features, including exercise guidance, performance tracking, and training reminders. We do not sell your personal information.

Data Storage and Sharing

Lifting Logger does not share your workout information with third parties for advertising or marketing. If future versions of the app introduce account, backup, or cloud-sync features, this policy will be updated before those features collect or transmit personal information.

Children's Privacy

Lifting Logger is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the app, please contact us so it can be addressed.

Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated as the app changes. The current version will always include its most recent update date.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact Max Blanksby at max@blanksby.com.
